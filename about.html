Our Mission

KEMT Center is a leading research institution dedicated to addressing global development challenges through rigorous, evidence-based research. Our work focuses on education, health, and economic development, with a particular emphasis on underserved communities.

  • Conducting large-scale field experiments in 12+ countries
  • Publishing in top-tier academic journals
  • Collaborating with governments and NGOs to inform policy

Our interdisciplinary team of researchers, data scientists, and policy experts works tirelessly to generate insights that drive meaningful change. We believe in the power of data to transform lives and create sustainable solutions.
