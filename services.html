Suivi de l'attrition, vérification rigoureuse de la conformité du traitement et double saisie sécurisée. Rigorous implementation of Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs) at the school, household, or community levels. Careful tracking of attrition, treatment compliance audits, and secure double data entries.

Advanced causal evaluations using quantitative econometrics in Stata and R (DID, RDD, Propensity Score Matching). Rigorous impact assessments of developmental and educational initiatives.
